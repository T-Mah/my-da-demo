Assumptions & Dependencies

  • Out of scope: Any sites not explicitly listed (regional, language, Privacy, HS, NIC, PMIS)
  • Content authoring access: SharePoint or Google Drive configured by Month 1, Week 3
  • Stakeholder reviews: Content/legal approvals don't exceed 1-week turnaround
  • Design parity: Visual fidelity targets are "functionally equivalent" not pixel-perfect redesign
  • Analytics/integrations: Third-party integrations (analytics, forms, CDN) are identified during Month 1 discovery

Risks to Monitor

RiskMitigation
MVP page set scope creep Lock MVP page list by end of Month 1, Week 2
Complex integrations on abbvie.com (forms, gated content) Identify in discovery; prototype in Month 2
Specialty sites (HS, NIC, PMIS) have unique compliance needs Early discovery in Month 4 to de-risk Phase 2
Regional branding deviations from boilerplate Define acceptable variance in Month 4 playbook
